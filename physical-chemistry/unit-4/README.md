## Generation and quality assurance
The 19-page source scan was inspected page by page. Ten source-figure extracts are inserted in the Word notes and indexed in the figure inventory. The 23-page A4 document was rendered to page images and visually checked. Word mathematics is stored as native editable Office Math (OMML): the current DOCX contains 303 native math objects, including 102 displayed equation blocks. Fractions use numerator/denominator bars, and multi-step derivations are broken into separate display lines. The previous hollow-square list markers have been removed.

## Learning support
Conceptual checkpoints and supplementary numerical practice questions follow the major topics. Answers are collected separately in Section 16, so the original five numerical questions on source page 19 remain distinct and unchanged.
